VinylSync Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Last Updated: December 5, 2025

Introduction

VinylSync ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how VinylSync handles your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Discogs Account Authentication

  • When you connect your Discogs account, we authenticate using OAuth 2.0
  • Your Discogs username is used to identify your account
  • We do not store your Discogs password

Collection and Wantlist Data

  • Album titles, artist names, release information
  • Collection folder organization
  • Wantlist items
  • Custom field values you've set in Discogs

Followed Users

  • Usernames of other Discogs users whose wantlists you follow
  • This list is stored locally on your device only

Information We Don't Collect

VinylSync does NOT collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identifying information (email, phone number, address)
  • Payment information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Browsing history
  • Device identifiers for advertising purposes

How We Use Your Information

Local Storage Only

All data retrieved from Discogs and Apple Music is:

  • Cached locally on your device for performance and offline access
  • Never transmitted to our servers (we don't have servers)
  • Never shared with third parties (except as described below)
  • Stored securely using iOS standard data protection

Authentication Credentials

Your Discogs OAuth tokens are:

  • Stored securely in the iOS Keychain
  • Protected by iOS encryption
  • Never transmitted except to Discogs for authentication
  • Automatically deleted when you sign out

Third-Party Services

VinylSync integrates with the following third-party services:

Discogs API

Purpose: To access your vinyl collection and wantlist data

Data Accessed: Your Discogs username, collection items, wantlist items, custom folders and fields, collection value statistics

Discogs Privacy Policy: https://www.discogs.com/privacy

Apple Music API (MusicKit)

Purpose: To search for albums and add them to your Apple Music library

Data Accessed: Apple Music catalog search results, your Apple Music library (only when you explicitly add albums)

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

StoreKit (In-App Purchases)

Purpose: To enable optional tipping/donations to support the app

Note: All payment processing is handled by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Data Storage and Security

Security Measures

  • OAuth 2.0: Secure authentication without storing passwords
  • SSL/TLS: All network communications are encrypted
  • Certificate Pinning: Additional security for API communications
  • No Cloud Storage: We don't use cloud services to store your data
  • No Analytics: No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs

Your Data Rights

Delete Your Data

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  1. Clear Cache: Settings → Clear Collection Cache (removes cached data, keeps you logged in)
  2. Sign Out: Settings → Sign Out (removes authentication credentials and all cached data)
  3. Delete App: Remove the app from your device (permanently deletes all app data)

Permissions

Camera Access (Optional)

  • Purpose: To scan barcodes on vinyl records for quick search
  • Required: No - you can use the app without granting camera access
  • Usage: Images are processed in real-time and never stored

Apple Music Access (Optional)

  • Purpose: To search for albums and add them to your Apple Music library
  • Required: No - Discogs features work without Apple Music
  • Usage: Only accesses Apple Music catalog and your library when you explicitly add albums

Children's Privacy

VinylSync is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do:

  • The "Last Updated" date at the top will be revised
  • If changes are significant, we will notify you through the app
  • Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy

Summary

✅What VinylSync Does:

  • Stores your Discogs collection data locally on your device
  • Uses secure OAuth for Discogs authentication
  • Caches data for offline use and performance
  • Protects credentials with iOS Keychain

❌What VinylSync Does NOT Do:

  • Store your data on external servers
  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Collect personal identifying information beyond username
  • Use analytics or advertising SDKs
  • Access your data without your explicit action

🔒Your Control:

  • All data stored locally on your device
  • Clear cache anytime in Settings
  • Sign out to remove all credentials
  • Protected by iOS security features
