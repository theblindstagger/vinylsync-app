About VinylSync

VinylSync is an iOS app that bridges the gap between your vinyl record collection and Apple Music. Connect your Discogs account to sync your collection and wantlist, then easily find and add albums to your Apple Music library.

Key Features

  • Sync your Discogs vinyl collection
  • Browse and search your collection offline
  • Find matching albums on Apple Music
  • Add albums to your Apple Music library with one tap
  • Track collection statistics and value
  • Follow other users' wantlists
  • Barcode scanning for quick searches

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Download VinylSync from the App Store, then sign in with your Discogs account. The app will sync your collection and you can start exploring right away.

Do I need a Discogs account?

Yes, VinylSync requires a Discogs account to access your vinyl collection data. You can create a free account at discogs.com.

Do I need Apple Music?

Apple Music is optional. You can browse and explore your Discogs collection without Apple Music. The Apple Music features (finding and adding albums) require an Apple Music subscription.

Is my data safe?

Yes! All your data is stored locally on your device. We don't have servers and don't collect your personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Why isn't my collection syncing?

Try these steps:

  1. Check your internet connection
  2. Make sure you're signed in to Discogs (Settings → Sign Out → Sign In)
  3. Try pulling to refresh on the Home screen
  4. Clear cache in Settings and sync again

How do I add albums to Apple Music?

Tap on any album in your collection, then tap "Find on Apple Music". Once the album is found, tap "Add to Library" to add it to your Apple Music collection.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes! Once your collection is synced, you can browse it offline. Searching Apple Music and syncing new items requires an internet connection.

How do I follow other users' wantlists?

Go to the Wantlist tab, tap the "+" button, and enter a Discogs username. Their wantlist will appear alongside yours.

Is VinylSync free?

Yes, VinylSync is free to use. There's an optional tip jar if you'd like to support development.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us at vinylsync.app@gmail.com. We appreciate all feedback and suggestions!

System Requirements

  • iOS 17.0 or later
  • iPadOS 17.0 or later
  • Internet connection for syncing
  • Discogs account (free)
  • Apple Music subscription (optional, for streaming features)

Troubleshooting

App crashes or freezes

  1. Make sure you're running the latest version from the App Store
  2. Try force-quitting and reopening the app
  3. Clear cache in Settings
  4. Restart your device
  5. If issues persist, contact support

Apple Music integration not working

  1. Verify you have an active Apple Music subscription
  2. Check that VinylSync has permission to access Apple Music (Settings → VinylSync → Media & Apple Music)
  3. Sign out and sign back in to Apple Music in the iOS Settings app

Barcode scanner not working

  1. Grant camera permission to VinylSync (Settings → VinylSync → Camera)
  2. Make sure there's adequate lighting
  3. Hold the barcode steady within the frame
